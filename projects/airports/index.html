World Airports

Where are the world’s airports? Every airport in the OpenFlights database, plotted on one map you can pan and zoom.

Interactive web map · MapLibre, Supercluster · Oct 2026

How to use it

  • Zoom in and the clusters split apart into smaller groups.
  • Click a cluster to zoom straight into it.
  • Click a single airport (yellow) to see its name, city and code.

How I built it

Thousands of points on one world map turn into a smudge. Supercluster groups nearby airports into clusters for each zoom level, so the map stays readable from the whole globe down to a single region. Darker, larger circles hold more airports.

Airport locations come from OpenFlights (opens in a new tab) (Open Database License), and country outlines from Natural Earth. The map is drawn with MapLibre.
