Xin Chào, welcome to
Thuận.

"Xin chào" means "Hello" and is pronounced like "seen chow" with careful attention to tones.

Xin: Pronounced like "seen" with a soft 's' sound, using mid-level tone without rising or falling inflection.

Chào: Pronounced like "chow" but with a falling tone, starting high and dropping down. The tone is crucial as it distinguishes this greeting from other words with similar sounds.
