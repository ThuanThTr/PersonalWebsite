bio

Background

I am an urban innovator based in the Greater Toronto Area. I am interested in how cities can become more inclusive, resilient, and people-centered through thoughtful policy, strong community engagement, and practical data analysis.

Education

Master of Urban Innovation from the University of Toronto, and a Bachelor of Arts (Honours) in Human Geography from McMaster University.

Work

I work at the intersection of urban strategy, public policy, and data to help cities and organizations design better services, stronger systems, and more sustainable futures.

Life

I care deeply about community, public life, and the everyday experiences that shape urban change. I enjoy being outdoors, learning from people and places, and thinking about how better design can improve everyday life.
